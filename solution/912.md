## 5. Complexity Analysis
    Time：O(n)
        
    Space: O(1)

**"The efficiency of quicksort depends on the balance of the partitions. If each partition is evenly balanced, the recursion depth is reduced to log n, resulting in an overall time complexity of O(n log n). If the partitions are unbalanced, the recursion depth can approach n, causing the total complexity to degrade to O(n²). Randomly selecting the pivot probabilistically ensures that each partition is approximately balanced, thereby avoiding the worst-case scenario."**
